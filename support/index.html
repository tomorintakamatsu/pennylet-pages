Getting started

Do I need an account or bank login?

No. Start with the money you can use now, the amount you want to protect, bills and savings before payday, and your next payday. Old bank history and CSV import are optional.

What is Safe Until Payday?

It starts with your spendable balance and cash, then adjusts for new activity and subtracts protected money, bills, savings, and subscriptions due before payday. Open “Why this number?” in the app to see the exact breakdown.

Do imported transactions change that number?

Transactions after your saved Today Snapshot can change it. Older transactions remain useful for reports but are not subtracted from money you already entered as today's balance.

Does payday add my salary automatically?

No. Payday tells PennyLet how long your current money must last. When your pay arrives, check the expected amount and tap Record Paycheck. Only a recorded payment increases your available money. You can allow a payday reminder on your phone.

Tracking tools

What does a watchlist do?

Spending Watch is a Pro feature. Tap Add, enter a merchant or category, then open it to see matching entries and this month's total. Online AI can suggest more matches when you allow it. Remove an incorrect match without deleting the original transaction, or delete the whole watchlist.

What is Review?

Review contains suggestions such as a possible transfer, refund, recurring payment, or missing category. Nothing is silently changed. Accept a correct suggestion or ignore it. Until then, the entry stays counted exactly as you added it.

Can PennyLet read every subscription in my Apple Account?

No. Apple only shares purchases associated with PennyLet. Add other subscriptions manually, or review recurring payments found in your saved transactions. You can open Apple's subscription settings to check or cancel subscriptions there.

How does receipt scanning work?

Choose or photograph a receipt, review the extracted details, then save the transaction. PennyLet reads text from the photo on your device. If you allow Online AI Processing, recognized text can be sent to PennyLet's online AI service to organize the result; the photo itself is not uploaded by the current flow.

AI and Pro

What does Pro add?

Pro adds Spending Watch, Ask PennyLet, monthly checks, forecasts, AI result charts, check reminders, and higher daily and weekly Money Check limits. Transactions, receipt scanning, custom categories, goals, subscriptions, themes, CSV tools, and private iCloud recovery remain available without Pro. Online AI requires your permission and an internet connection.

Does Ask PennyLet answer general questions?

No. It is limited to questions about data saved in PennyLet, such as spending, income, bills, subscriptions, goals, watchlists, and payday.

Can I turn online AI off?

Yes. Open Settings, then Legal, and turn off Online AI Processing. Money tracking and local calculations still work.

Data and subscriptions

Can my data come back after reinstalling?

Private iCloud Backup keeps dated saves. Check Settings > Data for a successful backup before deleting the app. Use Restore from iCloud to choose a save on a device signed into the same iCloud account. This is a recovery backup, not live sync.

What if backup fails?

Your local data stays in place. Check your connection, iCloud sign-in, and free iCloud storage, then tap Back Up Now. Do not delete the app until a backup succeeds. Restoring replaces data on that device, not your other saved backups.

What if Restore from iCloud cannot find my save?

Keep any local copies and do not reset again or turn backup off. Check that you are using the same iCloud account, then retry. If it still fails, contact support with the message shown in the app. Do not send your full financial export.

How do I export or reset?

Open Settings > Data. Export creates a transaction CSV, not a complete backup of settings, goals, or wallpapers. Reset clears this device and keeps your iCloud saves. Automatic backup stays paused until you restore or choose Save and Resume. Reset does not delete exported files or cancel Pro.

How does the trial work?

Eligible customers can try the yearly Pro subscription free for seven days. Monthly has no trial. Apple confirms eligibility and shows the price before purchase. The yearly subscription renews at the displayed price after the trial unless canceled. To avoid a trial charge, cancel at least 24 hours before the trial ends.

How do I restore Pro?

Use the Apple Account that purchased Pro, open Upgrade to Pro, and tap Restore Purchases. iCloud recovery does not grant Pro. An expired or refunded subscription cannot restore active access. If prices do not load, check your internet connection and App Store sign-in, then retry.

How do I cancel Pro?

Apple handles subscriptions. On iPhone, open Settings, tap your Apple Account, choose Subscriptions, select PennyLet, and choose Cancel Subscription.
