Getting started

Do I need an account or bank login?

No. Start with the money you can use now, the amount you want to protect, bills and savings before payday, and your next payday. Old bank history and CSV import are optional.

What is Safe Until Payday?

It starts with your spendable balance and cash, then adjusts for new activity and subtracts protected money, bills, savings, and subscriptions due before payday. Open “Why this number?” in the app to see the exact breakdown.

Do imported transactions change that number?

Transactions after your saved Today Snapshot can change it. Older transactions remain useful for reports but are not subtracted from money you already entered as today's balance.

Tracking tools

What does a watchlist do?

A watchlist gathers matching spending for one merchant or category you care about, such as coffee, dining, games, or transit. Open a watchlist to see the exact matching transactions. You can remove individual matches or delete the watchlist.

What is Review?

Review contains suggestions such as a possible transfer, refund, recurring payment, or missing category. Nothing is silently changed. Accept a correct suggestion or ignore it. Until then, the entry stays counted exactly as you added it.

How does receipt scanning work?

Choose or photograph a receipt, review the extracted details, then save the transaction. PennyLet reads text from the photo on your device. If you allow Online AI Processing, recognized text can be sent to PennyLet's online AI service to organize the result; the photo itself is not uploaded by the current flow.

AI and Pro

What does Pro add?

Pro adds Spending Watch, Ask PennyLet, monthly checks, forecasts, AI result charts, check reminders, and higher daily and weekly Money Check limits. Transactions, receipt scanning, custom categories, goals, subscriptions, themes, and CSV tools remain available without Pro.

Does Ask PennyLet answer general questions?

No. It is limited to questions about data saved in PennyLet, such as spending, income, bills, subscriptions, goals, watchlists, and payday.

Can I turn online AI off?

Yes. Open Settings, then Legal, and turn off Online AI Processing. Money tracking and local calculations still work.

Data and subscriptions

How do I export or reset?

Open Settings, then Data. Export creates a CSV you control. Reset removes PennyLet's local app data after confirmation. Export first if you want to keep transaction records.

How do I cancel Pro?

Apple handles subscriptions. On iPhone, open Settings, tap your Apple Account, choose Subscriptions, select PennyLet, and choose Cancel Subscription.
